## Main danger is dehydration
In diarrhoea and vomiting, the body loses water and salts. Dehydration is the main danger, especially in children and older adults.

## Oral rehydration solution (ORS)
Give ORS in small, frequent sips. Prepare it exactly as written on the packet, using clean boiled and cooled water. Continue normal feeding and breastfeeding. Do not make ORS stronger or weaker than the packet instructions.

## Signs of dehydration
Very dry mouth, sunken eyes, little or no urine, unusual sleepiness, and no tears in a crying child are warning signs. Seek medical care quickly if these appear.

## See a doctor
See a doctor if there is blood in the stool, black stool, fever with diarrhoea, vomiting that does not stop, diarrhoea lasting more than two days, or if the person is a baby, elderly or pregnant.
