## Call 108 immediately for
Chest pain or pressure, severe difficulty breathing, fainting or unconsciousness, signs of stroke (face drooping, arm weakness, slurred speech), seizures, severe bleeding that does not stop, vomiting or coughing blood, poisoning or overdose, snake bite, serious burns or head injury, severe allergic reaction with swelling of the face or throat, and thoughts of harming oneself.

## While waiting for help
Stay calm, stay with the person, keep them still and comfortable, and follow the instructions of the emergency operator. Do not give food or drink to an unconscious person.
