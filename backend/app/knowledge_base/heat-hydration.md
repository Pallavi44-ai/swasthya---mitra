## Staying safe in the heat
Drink water often even if you are not thirsty, avoid going out during the hottest hours, wear loose light-coloured cotton clothes and cover the head outdoors. Older adults, children and people with long-term illness are at higher risk.

## Heat stroke
Very hot dry skin, high body temperature, confusion, fainting or fits are signs of heat stroke, which is an emergency. Move the person to a cool place, loosen clothing, cool the body with wet cloths, and call 108.
