## Common cold and cough
Most colds and coughs are caused by viruses and improve by themselves in about a week. Rest, warm fluids, steam inhalation with care to avoid burns, and gargling with warm salt water can ease a sore throat and blocked nose. Antibiotics do not work against viruses and should only be taken if a doctor prescribes them.

## Protect others
Cover the mouth when coughing, wash hands often with soap, and avoid close contact with babies and elderly people while unwell.

## When to see a doctor
See a doctor if a cough lasts more than two to three weeks, if there is blood in the sputum, if breathing is difficult or noisy, if there is chest pain, or if fever continues for more than three days. A long cough with weight loss or night sweats should be checked for tuberculosis.
