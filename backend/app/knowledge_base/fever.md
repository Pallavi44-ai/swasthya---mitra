## What fever is
Fever means the body temperature is higher than normal, usually above about 38 °C (100.4 °F) when measured with a thermometer. Fever is a sign that the body is fighting something, most often an infection. It is a symptom, not a disease by itself.

## General care at home
Rest, drink plenty of fluids such as water, oral rehydration solution, soup or coconut water, and wear light clothing. A lukewarm sponge can be comfortable. Medicines to bring down fever should be used only as advised by a doctor or pharmacist, and the dose depends on age and weight.

## When to see a doctor
See a doctor if fever lasts more than three days, if it keeps returning, or if the person is very young, elderly, pregnant or has a long-term illness. Fever together with a rash, severe headache, stiff neck, confusion, difficulty breathing, repeated vomiting or very little urine needs urgent medical attention. In areas with mosquitoes, fever with body pain should be checked for dengue or malaria.
