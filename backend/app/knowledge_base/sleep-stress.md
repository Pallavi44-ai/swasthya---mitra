## Healthy routines
Regular sleep and wake times, daylight and gentle activity, limiting caffeine late in the day, and talking with family or friends can help with stress and poor sleep.

## When to seek help
If low mood, worry or sleeplessness lasts more than two weeks or affects daily life, speak to a doctor or counsellor. If you have thoughts of harming yourself, call Tele-MANAS on 14416 (free, 24x7) or 108 in immediate danger.
