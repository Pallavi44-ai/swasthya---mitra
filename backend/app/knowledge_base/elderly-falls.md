## Make the home safer
Keep floors dry and clutter-free, use good lighting including a night light on the way to the bathroom, fix loose mats, and use handrails and non-slip footwear. Ask a doctor to review medicines that cause dizziness or sleepiness.

## Stay active
Gentle walking and balance exercises as advised by a doctor or physiotherapist help keep muscles strong. Get eyesight checked regularly.

## After a fall
If there is severe pain, a head injury, inability to stand, or confusion after a fall, seek medical care urgently.
