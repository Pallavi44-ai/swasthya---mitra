## Prevention
Remove standing water around the home, use mosquito nets and repellents, and wear full sleeves in the evening. Cover water storage containers.

## Symptoms
Dengue typically causes sudden high fever with severe body ache, pain behind the eyes, headache and sometimes a rash. Malaria often causes fever with chills and shivering that may come in cycles. Both need testing by a doctor.

## Warning signs
Severe stomach pain, repeated vomiting, bleeding from the nose or gums, black stool, extreme tiredness, drowsiness or restlessness, and cold clammy skin after the fever settles need immediate medical care.
