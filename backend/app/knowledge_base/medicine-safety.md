## Take medicines as prescribed
Take each medicine exactly as written on the prescription or label: the right medicine, the right amount, at the right time, for the full number of days. Do not stop, skip or change medicines without asking your doctor. Do not share your medicines with others.

## Check before you start
Tell your doctor and pharmacist about your allergies, other medicines, herbal products, pregnancy and long-term illnesses. If a prescription is hard to read, ask the pharmacist or doctor to confirm it instead of guessing.

## Missed dose
Instructions for a missed dose are different for each medicine. Ask your pharmacist or doctor, and do not take a double dose unless a health professional tells you to.

## Storage and expiry
Keep medicines in a cool, dry place away from children. Check expiry dates and do not use expired medicines.
