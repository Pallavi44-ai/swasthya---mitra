## Wash hands
Wash hands with soap and water for at least 20 seconds before eating or preparing food, after using the toilet, and after coughing or sneezing.

## Safe water and food
Drink boiled or treated water, keep food covered, wash fruits and vegetables, cook food thoroughly, and refrigerate leftovers promptly. These steps prevent many stomach infections.
