## What it is
High blood pressure often has no symptoms, which is why it is called a silent condition. Over the years it can damage the heart, brain, kidneys and eyes. It is diagnosed only by repeated blood pressure measurements by a health worker.

## Living well with it
Eating less salt, being physically active, keeping a healthy weight, avoiding tobacco and limiting alcohol all help. Blood pressure medicines must be taken regularly exactly as prescribed, even when you feel well. Never stop or change them without asking your doctor.

## Seek urgent help
Chest pain, severe headache, sudden weakness, trouble speaking, vision problems or breathlessness with high blood pressure need emergency care. Call 108.
