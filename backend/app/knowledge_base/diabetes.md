## What it is
Diabetes means there is too much sugar in the blood because the body cannot make or use insulin properly. Common symptoms are excessive thirst, frequent urination, tiredness, blurred vision, slow-healing wounds and unexplained weight loss.

## Daily care
Follow the diet, exercise and medicine plan given by your doctor. Check your feet daily for cuts or sores, keep regular follow-up visits, and get eyes and kidneys checked as advised. Do not change diabetes medicine or insulin on your own.

## Low blood sugar
Shakiness, sweating, hunger, confusion or fainting can mean blood sugar is too low, especially in people taking insulin or some diabetes tablets. If the person is awake and can swallow, a sugary drink or glucose may help, then seek medical advice. If the person is unconscious or cannot swallow, call 108 at once.
