## Common headaches
Most headaches are tension-type or related to tiredness, stress, dehydration, hunger, poor sleep or long screen time. Rest in a quiet place, drink water, eat regular meals, and keep a regular sleep routine. Painkillers should be used only as advised by a doctor or pharmacist.

## Warning signs
Seek urgent medical help for a sudden, very severe headache, headache after a head injury, headache with fever and stiff neck, confusion, weakness on one side, difficulty speaking, vision loss or fits. See a doctor if headaches are frequent, keep getting worse, or wake you from sleep.
